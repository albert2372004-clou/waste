What is the Need for the Circular Innovation Fund?

Industrial circular economy cannot function on simple scrap trading alone. Raw industrial byproducts like discarded vulcanized tyres, raw coconut husks, and marine fish scales cannot be used directly by factories without pre-processing.

⚙️ 1. The Pre-Processing Machinery Barrier

To convert scrap tyres into road bitumen additive, facilities require specialized cryogenic pulverizers. Converting fish scales into biomedical collagen requires enzymatic peptide extractors. Small MSME recyclers in Kerala lack the capital to purchase this industrial equipment.

🤝 2. Enterprise Co-Investment & Micro-Pledges

The Circular Fund enables large buyers (tyre producers, infrastructure developers, activated carbon kilns) to pool micro-grants and back local processing units. In return, contributing buyers secure priority off-take agreements.

🌿 3. 100% Zero-Landfill Closed Loop

By funding local conversion machinery in Kottayam, Kochi, and Palakkad, byproduct streams avoid open dumping. Instead, they re-enter Kerala's industrial production lines as certified secondary raw materials.
